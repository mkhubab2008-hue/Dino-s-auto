# Dino-s-auto
I automated the dino game on Google chrome with my Arduino uno a servo motor and an ldr

# Parts Used
I used an arduino uno as the brains of this project, an ldr to detect when an obstacle came in the dino's path, a 180 degree sg90 servo motor to press the space bar at just the right time, jumper wires to connect the 
diferrent components and a usb cable to connect the arduino to my computer.
# Wiring
I connected the vcc pins of the servo and the ldr module to the arduino's 5v pins, the GND pins of the servo to the arduinos gnd, the signal pins of the servo and ldr to D8 and A0 respectively on the arduino.
